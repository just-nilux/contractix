Employment Offer Letter

Fictional demonstration document. Aurora Ledger Technologies UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Prinzessinnenstraße 3, 10969 Berlin (the "Company") is pleased to offer employment to Mr. Jonas Example (the "Employee") on the terms below.

1. Position and Start Date

The Employee will serve as Staff Engineer, Payments Infrastructure, reporting to the VP of Engineering. The employment starts on 15 September 2026 and is entered into for an indefinite term. The place of work is Berlin, with up to two remote days per week.

2. Base Salary

The Employee will receive an annual gross base salary of EUR 110,000, payable in twelve equal monthly installments. The salary will be reviewed annually; a review does not imply an increase.

3. Signing Bonus

The Employee will receive a one-time signing bonus of EUR 8,000 gross, payable with the first regular payroll. If the Employee resigns within twelve months of the start date, the signing bonus must be repaid in full.

4. Equity Award

Subject to approval by the Company's shareholders, the Employee will be granted 20,000 virtual share options under the Company's Virtual Share Option Program with a strike price of EUR 0.85 per option. The options vest over four years with a one-year cliff: 25 % vest on the first anniversary of the vesting commencement date, and the remainder vests in equal monthly installments over the following 36 months. Unvested options lapse on termination of employment for any reason.

5. Working Hours

Regular working time is 40 hours per week. The salary under Section 2 compensates reasonable overtime up to ten hours per week; this is expressly agreed to be covered by the base salary.

6. Vacation

The Employee is entitled to 26 working days of paid vacation per calendar year.

7. Probation and Notice

The first six months constitute a probationary period, during which either party may terminate with two weeks' notice. Thereafter, the Employee may terminate with one month's notice to the end of a calendar month, and the Company may terminate in accordance with statutory notice periods.

8. Intellectual Property

The Employee assigns to the Company all rights, title and interest in and to any inventions, works of authorship, software, designs, and other work product conceived, created or reduced to practice during the term of employment, whether or not created during working hours or using Company equipment, to the maximum extent permitted by law. The Employee agrees to execute all documents reasonably necessary to perfect such rights. No carve-out for personal or open-source side projects is granted under this agreement.

9. Confidentiality

The Employee shall keep confidential all trade secrets and confidential business information of the Company and its customers, during and after the employment, without time limitation.

10. Exclusivity

The Employee will devote their full working capacity to the Company. Any secondary employment requires the Company's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

11. Governing Law

This offer and the employment relationship are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany. The German-language employment contract to be executed at signing prevails over this letter in case of conflict.

Aurora Ledger Technologies UG
Jonas Example
